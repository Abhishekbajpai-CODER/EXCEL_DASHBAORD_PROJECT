# EXCEL_DASHBAORD_PROJECT
**AZ Executive Summary Profitability Dashboard**

This project showcases proficiency in end-to-end data analysis, transforming transactional data into a dynamic reporting tool. The dashboard leverages advanced Excel techniques, including pivot tables, VLOOKUPs where applicable, and complex DAX or formulas to track profitability, revenue trends, and regional performance seamlessly. It demonstrates data storytelling and the ability to build tools that facilitate data-driven decision-making for stakeholders.

**Project Features**

**Monthly Revenue and Profit Trends:** A line chart visualizing the monthly progression of total revenue and total gross profit.
**Top 5 Countries by Revenue:** A bar chart highlighting the highest-performing countries based on revenue generation.
**Revenue Share by Channel:** A pie chart breaking down the revenue distribution across different sales channels.
**Region-wise Comparison:** A doughnut chart illustrating regional performance comparisons.
**Geographical Insights**: A map chart providing visual context for country-wise revenue data.
**Interactive Slicers:** Dynamic filters for Customer Type, Product Category, Region, Country, Year, Quarter, and Month to enable in-depth data exploration
